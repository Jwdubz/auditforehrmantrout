What your site is costing you — Ehrmantrout Orthodontics —

I put together an audit of the tweaks I'd make, plus a quick look at how your business shows up when someone searches for what you do. I went through ehrmantroutorthodontics.com the way a parent or an adult who just searched for braces or Invisalign in Las Vegas would: land on a page, see who the doctor is and what it costs to start, then call or ask for a consultation. The foundation is strong: all 35 pages in your sitemap load, each has one headline and its own title and description, your hours and phone number are in the business markup, the homepage loads quickly on a simulated phone, and five patient reviews sit on your homepage. The leaks are in the details: three different suite numbers for your office across the web, white text on your teal buttons that is hard to read, a floating button that covers your headline and form, a phone number that breaks across two lines on a phone, reviews on one page out of 35, and form choices that don't match your listed hours.

Checked October 23, 2026

What I found

01

Your office shows three different suite numbers online.

Your website's footer, contact section and business markup give 6889 S Eastern Ave, Suite 103. The Better Business Bureau quote page for Ehrmantrout Orthodontics lists 6889 S Eastern Ave Ste 110A, and the LinkToReview page, which a web search returned, lists 6889 S Eastern Ave Ste 100 a. The street number is the same on all three. I can't tell which suite is right, or what your Google Business Profile says, because I didn't open it. I'm only reporting that the three pages disagree. I read all three on October 23, 2026.

02

The white text on your teal buttons is hard to read.

Lighthouse flagged 40 elements on your homepage for low contrast, and 8 on your request page. Buttons such as “Schedule a Consult”, “Start Your Smile Journey”, “Get Started Today!” and “Call Us At: (725) 204-0288” are white (#ffffff) on teal (#0ec9b4), a ratio of 2.09 to 1, and the “Send” button on the request form is white on #0bb39f at 2.63 to 1. The usual minimum for text this size is 4.5 to 1. Your teal headings (2.09 to 1 on white, 1.93 on cream), the teal “All areas we serve” links (2.44 on cream) and the grey text under the contact headings (3.58 to 3.87) were flagged too. This was one run of one tool on each page, and I can't tell from outside whether it keeps anyone from tapping.

03

The “Pay over time” button covers your headline and your form.

In a 1440 by 900 desktop window on your request page, the floating “Pay over time” button sits over the end of the headline “Request Your Appointment”. On a 390-pixel-wide phone window it covers the “Early Care” tab on the homepage's first screen and the label “Are you a new patient?” on the request page. I only checked those two pages at those two sizes, and I can't tell how it looks on other windows or real phones. Screenshots are saved.

04

On a phone, your phone number breaks across two lines.

In the green bar at the top of the page, at 390 pixels wide, “Call Us At: (725) 204-0288” wraps so that “(725) 204-” sits on one line and “0288” on the next. I saw it on the homepage and the request page. The link is still tappable (167 by 45 pixels), so this is about how it reads, not whether it works. I loaded only those two pages at phone width, and I can't tell how it looks on a real phone.

05

Your patient reviews are on one page out of 35.

The homepage shows five reviews, from Kulitz, Jennifer, Kimberly, Nicole and Sunshine. I found no patient review text on the other 34 pages, including the Invisalign, braces, early care, request and area pages. The homepage section headed “Actual Results, Real Patients” shows four numbers (15+ years, 9000+ cases, 98%, 12+ areas) and no patient photos, and I found no before and after photos on any of the 35 pages. Outside the site, the LinkToReview page a web search returned shows 5.0 from 12 reviews, combined from Google and LinkToReview, while the same set of searches showed Las Vegas Orthodontics at 4.9 from 449 reviews on Birdeye. I can't see your Google profile, so I can't tell your live Google count.

06

The request form offers times your listed hours don't cover.

The form asks for a preferred day (Any, then Monday through Saturday) and a preferred time (Any, Early Morning, Morning, Afternoon, Evening). Your pages and your business markup list Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM. Saturday, and possibly Early Morning and Evening, fall outside those hours. I can't tell whether you see patients then or how those requests are handled, so this is a question rather than a fault.

What's working

The site is well built. All 35 pages in your sitemap return 200, each has exactly one headline, a title and description of its own, and a canonical address that is its own, and I found no image missing alt text. Your business markup (Dentist, Physician and FAQ) carries your address, hours, phone number and social profiles, and the phone number matches the one on the pages. Lighthouse, one run each on a simulated phone, scored your homepage 97 for performance with the main content at 2.4 seconds, your request page 99 and your Summerlin page 94. Every page shows the same phone number as a tap-to-call link, your doctor and office are named plainly, and the Paradise and Summerlin area pages, the two I read closely, carry local details such as roads and neighborhoods. Your robots.txt blocks only /thank-you/ and /styleguide/, and it lists your sitemap.

Unmeasured

Rankings, visitor numbers, your Google Business Profile as it appears today (the 5.0 from 12 reviews comes from a web search result, and I didn't open Google), your Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube pages, whether the request form reaches you every time, how many people tap the text number in your email, and how the pages look on a real phone: I tested widths in a desktop browser only. I loaded your homepage, request page and Summerlin page in a browser, and read the HTML of all 35 pages in your sitemap.

What I'd fix first

Pick the right suite number and make your site, your Google profile, the BBB page and LinkToReview match. Use a darker text color on the teal buttons, or a darker teal, so the labels are easy to read. Move the “Pay over time” button so it stops covering the headline and the form, and let the phone number sit on one line in the top bar. Put two or three of your homepage reviews on the Invisalign, braces and request pages, and add patient photos if you have permission. Check that the form's day and time choices match when you see patients. I can't promise where any of it will place you.
